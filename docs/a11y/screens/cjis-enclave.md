# CJIS enclave screens and the console's enclave panel — accessibility record

Screens: in the enclave app (`apps/web-cjis`, https://localhost:8443) the system use notification, the passkey re-sign-in prompt, home, records list, the record page (people involved, weapons, body-camera references, narratives and amendments, disposition, review, timeline), the confirmation dialog, accounts and screening with the one-time password hand-over, approvals, audit review, retention and legal holds, the lock screen; in the console the mission page's "Law enforcement (CJIS)" panel and the missions list's "Law enforcement" column. Keycloak's own sign-in pages are the identity provider's theme and are recorded separately when the enclave theme is customised. Layer 5, 2026-10-14.
