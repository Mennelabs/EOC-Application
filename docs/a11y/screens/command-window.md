# Command window: accessibility record

Layer 7, tasks C1–C5, recorded 2026-10-15 (task J). Three ways in, one rule underneath: plain words or shorthand become a plan, a person confirms the plan, and every essential task still has its own screen (CMD-20–CMD-34, AC-C11, AI-10).

- **Console** (`apps/web-console/src/ui/command-window.tsx`), in the header of every workspace:
  - an always-visible "Ask or command" button with its shortcut announced (`aria-keyshortcuts`: Ctrl+K or Command+K; "/" outside text fields; the letter and "/" can be changed on the device);
  - a native `<dialog>` named by its heading, with focus on the command field when it opens and back on the opener when it closes;
  - a choice between matches as a radio group with the legend "Which one?";
  - the plan with its effect, how a mistake is put right, and Confirm, Cancel and Undo (with its deadline);
  - links to the records it touched;
  - one polite live region announcing each step;
  - "Your recent commands" with "Clear my history" and a shared-device switch, and the "Aliases" panel;
  - when a screen shows one mission or need, a checkbox naming it ("Let "this" mean need …, open on your screen"), checked until the person unchecks it (CMD-06);
  - when a change would touch a record with unsaved text in a form on the screen, a group named "Unsaved changes" that names the form and offers "Keep my changes and go back to them" (focus goes to that form) or "Discard my changes and confirm"; there is no plain Confirm until one is chosen (CMD-29, AC-C09).
- **Field app** (`apps/field/app/command.tsx`): the screen "Ask or command". Words come from the keyboard's microphone or typing; the plan is confirmed on screen; nothing is kept on the phone.
- **Find help** (`apps/web-help`): the resident's "Ask a question" box. Fixed rules, no model; it answers with published places and links.
