# Console frame, home and briefing mode: accessibility record

Screens: the console's frame on every workspace (`apps/web-console/src/App.tsx` with `@cg/ui`'s `TopBar`, `SideNav`, `PersonMenu`, `Shell`), the home (`/console/`, `apps/web-console/src/screens/home.tsx`: the situation page for roles with the dashboard, the work page for everyone else) and briefing mode (`/console/briefing`). Design foundation, 2026-10-15 (ADR-0045). They have:

- the top bar: the product and county (a link home), the open event's chip (name, hazard, state, time since it opened), the command-window trigger (Ctrl+K), the person's menu (theme, density, briefing mode, sign out);
- the workspaces down the side, grouped under headings, only those the person's permissions answer for, with a count beside a link where there is something waiting; on narrow screens a "Workspaces" button in the top bar opens them as a drawer;
- the situation home: key counts as tiles with their as-of time and source, the declaration window, "Needs your decision" (recommendations, facility proposals, permission changes, overdue reminders), significant events, links to the map and the reports;
- the work home: the person's workspaces as cards;
- briefing mode: the situation page at 150% type, refreshed every minute, without the decision list, with a "Leave briefing mode" link.
