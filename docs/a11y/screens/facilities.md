# Facility registry, approvals and the public offer form: accessibility record

Layer 7, tasks D2 and D3, recorded 2026-10-15 (task J). Three screens:

- **Facility registry** (`/console/facilities`, `apps/web-console/src/screens/facilities.tsx`):
  - a table captioned "Facilities", with what is known and unknown and whether the re-check is due or stale, plus filters;
  - one entry with its contacts (shown only to roles that may see them) and "Confirm still right";
  - the form "Add a facility" / "Change …". County EM saves it directly; other roles send it for approval.
- **Facility approvals** (`/console/facility-approvals`):
  - a table captioned "Proposals waiting for a decision", each proposal with what it changes;
  - "Decide …" with a decision and a reason.
- **Offer a place for disaster use** (`apps/web-help/src/offer.ts`): the public form linked from Find help, in English and Spanish. Each question is a radio group with a legend; anything unknown can stay blank.
