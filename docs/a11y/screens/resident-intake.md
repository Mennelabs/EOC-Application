Open before GATE-03 can be claimed in full: check 2 (a screen-reader pass by a person) and the professional Spanish review (EQ-07). The automated evidence runs on every push.

CHN-09 (2026-10-16): **my report** gains "Add photos", a section with its own heading, the count of photos on the report in words, and a labelled file input ("Add a photo"). Saving is announced in a status line ("Photo added…"), a failure in an alert that says the report is safe. Photos are prepared on the device as in intake. `tests/e2e/resident.spec.ts` adds a photo there and runs axe, on desktop and phone.

Layer 6, task I (2026-10-15): **my report** gains "Download my copy (PDF)" (FL:FR-32). The button is reached and pressed by keyboard in `tests/e2e/resident.spec.ts`; the outcome is a status line. The PDF is tagged PDF/UA-1 in English and Spanish (veraPDF on `resident-copy-en.pdf` and `resident-copy-es.pdf` in CI): headings, the answers as two-column tables with headers in the reader's language, and each photo with alternative text naming the photo and what it shows. The screen-reader pass of the PDF is pending a person, with the rest of check 2.

Layer 7 (2026-10-15): dictation on the own-words step no longer uses the browser's speech recognition (which sends audio to Google or Apple). Where the county has switched on `intake.transcription`, a "Speak instead" button (a toggle with `aria-pressed`) records on the device. "Stop and write it down" sends the clip to the county's AI service, and the words come back into the same labelled text box to read and change. A plain sentence under the button says a computer writes down the words and the recording is not kept (AIG-10). One status line announces "Recording…" and then "Writing down what you said…". A refused microphone or a failed call is said in words, with typing always open. The button is hidden when the county has no AI, when offline, and when the resident turned computer help off. `resident.spec.ts` checks that it is absent without the service; the recording itself needs a person with a device (check 2 and a device pass).

Layer 7 L64 (2026-10-16, ORG-01): the welcome screen gains a fourth start button, "I am reporting for a business, landlord or group" (Spanish "Reporto para un negocio, arrendador o grupo"), in the same stacked button group. After an organization's place is received, the confirmation shows a card headed "Other places for <organization>". When more than one place was reported, it lists them as "Places reported in this visit (2):" with the reference numbers as text. A sentence says each place gets its own number and what is filled in (or, once the device was cleared, that the contact will be asked again). The card ends with the primary button "Report another place for <organization>". The next place starts at the safety question with focus on the main region, like any report. `resident.spec.ts` walks two places by keyboard, on desktop and phone, with axe on both confirmations; screen reader pass still pending a person.

BRD:FR-01.06 (2026-10-16): **my report** gains "Lost your reference number?" (Spanish "¿Perdió su número de referencia?"), a link-styled button under the lookup form. It opens a screen in place, headed "Get my reference number again", with focus moved to its start. The screen has one labelled field (the phone or email on the report) and the button "Send my reference number". The outcome is a status line that never shows the number: it says the number is on its way to that phone or email. "Back to checking my report" returns to the lookup. `resident.spec.ts` walks it by keyboard with axe on both states, desktop and phone; the screen-reader pass is pending a person with the rest of check 2.
