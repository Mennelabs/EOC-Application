# AI governance mapped to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework

This document maps the platform's AI governance to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0, NIST AI 100-1) and the Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1), so a county, state or NGO can reuse it in a procurement or security review (AIG-11). Written at the end of layer 7 (2026-10-15) from the AI use register, the code and the tests. Every row names what the platform does, where the evidence is, and what is left to the buyer or to Mennelabs as process rather than software.

## The rule underneath every AI feature

**AI suggests, drafts and interprets; a named person decides; every essential task works with AI switched off** (AI-01, AI-06, AI-10, RULE-13). No feature decides eligibility, priority, a damage category or a dispatch. The provider for every AI function is OpenAI under the Mennelabs organization's zero-data-retention terms, until a government-cloud provider is chosen (decided 2026-10-15, ADR-0043); the interfaces are provider-neutral (AIG-12, ADR-0025).

## The AI features (AIG-01)

The register lists 17 features (`packages/ai/src/registry.ts`, `AI_FEATURES`). Each has the data sent, the decision it informs, the human checkpoint, the owner, the provider, the model and the evaluation. Each tenant can see and change its own copy at **Console → AI use register** (`/ai-register`):

| Area                | Features                                                                                          | Human checkpoint                                                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Resident intake     | `intake.extraction`, `intake.transcription`                                                       | The resident keeps, changes or drops each suggested answer; staff review                        |
| Review              | `review.translation`, `photo.quality`, `photo.sensitive_regions`, `photo.severity`                | A credentialed reviewer sets the degree; blur fails closed                                      |
| Field               | `field.voice_draft`, `scribe.transcription`, `scribe.note`, `field.handover`, `field.translation` | The worker confirms the note or hand-over; consent, legal and medical go to a human interpreter |
| Command window      | `command.interpret`                                                                               | Every change is a plan the person confirms; undo inside its window                              |
| Drafting and public | `report.drafting`, `declaration.drafting`, `public.drafting`, `public.speech`                     | A person edits and saves; public text is published by a person                                  |
| Discovery           | `discovery.web_search`                                                                            | A person accepts, edits or rejects each candidate with a reason                                 |

Every new feature ships **off**. An administrator switches it on with a reason after the provider passes the evaluation for the tenant's languages. `photo.severity` stays off even when the evaluation passes.

## Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1) risks

| Risk                                           | Control                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Confabulation                                  | **Drafts.** Every sentence cites the facts it uses, and a figure not in the facts refuses the draft (`checkDraft`).<br>**Scribe notes.** A line whose quoted words were not said is dropped.<br>**Hand-overs.** These are checked against the shift's own records.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Information integrity                          | **Labels.** Public text, audio and social cards are labelled as drafted or computer-generated, and a person publishes them.<br>**Discovery.** A web search result is a candidate with its source, decided by a person.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Data privacy                                   | **Before the model.** Text is redacted first.<br>**The provider.** It keeps nothing (zero retention).<br>**Recordings.** Scribe audio is deleted on confirmation, and unconfirmed notes expire after 30 days.<br>**Translation.** Nothing of the words is stored.<br>**CJIS.** The enclave has no AI.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Information security (prompt injection)        | **Data, not instructions.** Untrusted text is passed as delimited data (AIG-02); a closing tag inside the text is stripped.<br>**Fixed schemas.** Extraction returns a fixed schema, validated on the server (AIG-03).<br>**No credentials.** The command window's model holds none: every action goes through the catalog with server-side authorization (AIG-04, CMD-11, CMD-12).<br>**Detection.** Injection attempts produce no output and are counted in the evaluation. |
| Harmful bias or homogenization                 | **Monitoring.** Agreement is reported by group, with alerts (PHO-13). Languages are evaluated separately.<br>**Rules.** Recommendations carry an equity check, and need signals outrank AI.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Human-AI configuration                         | **Who acts.** Confirmation is bound to the plan, with undo windows.<br>**Over-reliance.** Reviewers set the degree from the FEMA matrix whether or not a suggestion exists. The suggestion appears after the matrix, and agreement is recorded so over-reliance can be seen.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Value chain and component integration          | **Pluggable.** The provider is pluggable, with per-output provenance (provider, model, prompt version).<br>**Hosting.** Hosting moves to an authorized government cloud.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Dangerous, violent, hateful or obscene content | **Filtering.** Output filtering is applied to public text (AIG-07), and a person publishes everything public.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Intellectual property                          | **Content.** Generated text is drafted from the county's own records.<br>**Sources.** Discovery cites its sources.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| CBRN information; environmental impact         | **Not in scope.** No feature produces such content. Calls are recorded, so their volume can be reported.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Known gaps (for the buyer's review)

- **Photo-model fairness before release (AIG-08).** The evaluation suite covers text extraction, injection and the command window, but has no labelled damage-photo set.
  - The severity suggestion therefore starts off. When switched on, its fairness is measured from reviewers' decisions in production, by hazard, home type and income band (PHO-13), with alerts.
  - A labelled photo set (synthetic or licensed) with fairness slices is needed before the suggestion is relied on at scale. The validated classifier from the university partnership would bring one.
- **Automatic switch-off (AIG-09).** Replaced by alerts on Adam's decision of 2026-10-15; a person switches a feature off.
- **Re-evaluation on model change (AIG-08).** Running the suite again is an administrator's action. Nothing yet triggers it automatically when the provider's model name changes.
- **Resident complaints (AIG-09).** These are not yet counted as a health signal.
- **Screen-reader passes** for the AI panels are pending a person (GATE-03).
