Who uses which feature

Every persona in the requirement docs mapped to the thirteen feature areas of release 1, with the requirement IDs that put them there. Personas come from the console cast in docs/02, the frontline roles in docs/04 (FR-01 to FR-32), and the five user groups in docs/design/users-and-needs.md. Feature areas follow the SCL-14 build order.

A filled cell means that area is the persona's home workspace or main tool. A lighter cell means they contribute to or use it as part of other work. An outlined cell means they only read from it, usually a consented or aggregate view. A struck cell is a guardrail: the docs say no access.

How the personas meet around one survivor record

The feature areas are not separate products. They all read and write one consented survivor record (FL-03, "tell it once"), which is why a resident never repeats their story across the twelve hand-offs in the WESTWIND exercise.

Read it by row

A row with one filled cell is a persona the product serves with a single screen (legal aid, counselors, the state liaison). A row with several is a role that moves between the field app, the survivor record and the console.

Read it by column

The survivor record and frontline capture column (F9) is the widest: 19 personas use it and one is barred from it. The intake column has three. That is the design argument for "tell it once" (FL-03).

Guardrails are features too

Struck cells are requirements, not gaps: no criminal-justice data outside the enclave (SEC-12), no scribe for counselors (FL-05), no access for insurers (FR-32). They cannot be switched on per county (ADM-05).
