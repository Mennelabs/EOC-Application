## 3. APIs (staff API under `/api/v1`, all through the policy engine)

- **Missions:** create (draft-save and intake-save rules apart from dispatch prerequisites, FR-02.02), list and filter, read with timeline, update with If-Match, transitions with the allowed graph (cancel and reopen need a reason and confirmation), priority change with reason and confirmation, lock and unlock, people involved (restricted fields masked by role), narratives and amendments, disposition and closure with prerequisite checks (AC-07C), media references.
- **Assignments:** assign one or more resources in one transaction with the mission (MIS-04, AC-06A); conflict checks (double-booked, off duty, over hours, missing credential, inactive person) answered as warnings with an authorized override and a reason (PER-02, AC-05B, AC-06C); dispatch through a method; acknowledge; status facts; release; reopen.
- **Personnel, teams, credentials:** credentials with verification and expiry; skill search that returns only recorded qualifications (AC-05A); teams and members; self-update versus coordinator update (FR-05.04).
- **Check-in and devices:** check in by staging QR or event code, check out; device registration and remote wipe; positions ingested in batches only while checked in.
- **Dispatch:** board snapshot (teams with status, last position at the tenant's precision, open work, hazards, sites); unassigned work queue sorted by priority; suggestion of the nearest qualified available team with its reasons; team status; check-in timers; safety alerts and acknowledgement; messages and receipts; net control log; activity and significant events; replay of a time window (DSP-12).
- **Live updates:** `GET /api/v1/stream` (Server-Sent Events, ADR-0022) fed by a RabbitMQ fan-out exchange. Each event carries the record type, id and version; the client refetches through the normal API, so the stream never bypasses authorization.
- **Field sync:** `GET /api/v1/field/sync?cursor=` returns changes for the caller's scopes since the cursor; `POST /api/v1/field/commands` applies a batch of client commands in order with the same validation, authorization and idempotency as the console; a stale version answers 409 with the current record; append-only facts never conflict. `GET /api/v1/field/area` returns the area package: assignments, parcels, hazards, sites, job aids and a map extract when tiles exist.
- **Hazards, reminders, badges:** CRUD with state histories; badge print view, print requested, confirmed or failed by a person, revoke, replace; `POST /api/v1/badges/verify` for checkpoints (FR-03) answers valid, expired, revoked or unknown and never shows more than name, agency and access label.
- **Role records:** one small route set per record in §2, each audited, restricted fields masked by data category.
- **Imports and exports (CON-17, SCL-13):** assessments out as Survey123-compatible CSV and GeoJSON; routes and parcels in from GeoJSON or CSV.

## 5. Field app architecture (ADR-0014, refined in ADR-0032)

- **Expo SDK 57** with local builds (`expo prebuild`, Gradle and Xcode); expo-router; TypeScript strict. EAS cloud builds stay off (a paid service).
- **One shared core:** `packages/field-core` holds the outbox, the sync loop, conflict handling, parcel snapping, USNG conversion, street-sweep ordering, check-in timers, the battery policy and the personal-data purge as plain TypeScript tested with Vitest. The question-graph engine from `packages/intake` runs unchanged for assisted intake (CHN-06).
- **Storage behind an adapter:** on phones, op-sqlite with SQLCipher and the key in the Keychain or Keystore through expo-secure-store, unlocked by PIN or biometrics; on the web build, IndexedDB with AES-GCM under a key derived from the PIN. The web build exists so the same screens run in the browser tests with axe (react-native-web); the release is the native app.
- **Location:** expo-location with a background task only while checked in (Q-07); the battery saver lowers the frequency; the sharing indicator is always visible while it runs; positions go through the outbox as append-only facts.
- **Maps:** MapLibre React Native on phones and maplibre-gl on the web build, reading the area extract cut from the self-hosted PMTiles by the worker (`pmtiles extract` with the area's bounding box); without tiles, parcels and pins still work on a plain background (SCL-06, as on the public page).
- **Sign-in:** OIDC with PKCE against the tenant's realm through expo-auth-session; refresh token in secure storage; the app locks after inactivity.
- **Device management:** managed app configuration (tenant URL, single-app mode) read by a small local Expo module; remote wipe clears only the app's data on the next contact; screenshots blocked on screens with personal details (expo-screen-capture).
- **Builds in CI:** the web build and its browser tests run on every push; an Android debug build (`expo prebuild` and Gradle on the Linux runner) proves the native project compiles. iOS needs a macOS runner (§14).

## 6. Dispatch, safety and privacy rules

1. Assignment, dispatch, delivery, acknowledgement, travel and arrival are separate facts (RULE-03). "Arrived" and "left" are suggested from location and confirmed by the team (DSP-05).
2. An unacknowledged assignment raises an alert after 10 minutes, tenant-set (DSP-04).
3. Check-in timer per team, default 60 minutes; a miss turns the team overdue and alerts the dispatcher, then the safety officer after a tenant-set delay (default 15 minutes) (DSP-07).
4. Help alerts carry the exact position whatever the precision setting, take over every dispatcher's screen until acknowledged, and are queued on the phone until a network appears.
5. Teams heading into a newly marked hazard area are warned on the phone and on the board (FLD-16, DSP-07).
6. Positions are stored only while checked in and seen only by dispatch and safety roles at the tenant's precision (default exact for those roles, nothing for others); trails are dropped after 30 days (DSP-08).
7. Each agency's dispatchers see and assign their own people; the county coordinator sees everyone; rules by role, agency or person as on the needs board (DSP-09, CON-15).
8. Every assignment, status change, message, check-in and alert writes an ICS 214 activity entry and, where significant, the event log (DSP-11).

## 12. Ambiguities resolved in favour of safety and privacy (flagged for Adam)

1. **A help alert always carries the exact position**, whatever the precision setting, and stays on every dispatcher's screen until a person acknowledges it.
2. **Location sharing stops at check-out**, and the app shows the sharing indicator the whole time it runs; there is no hidden mode.
3. **"Arrived" is never set automatically.** The app suggests it from location; the team confirms (RULE-03, DSP-05).
4. **Resident details leave the phone** once the server has them, and at check-out; only the volunteer's own assignments are on the phone (FLD-14).
5. **Remote wipe touches only the app's data**, on personal and issued phones alike (FLD-02).
6. **Children:** no photos by default, the minimum data, and a guardian pickup code that is printed and spoken as well as scanned (FR-26, ACC-50).
7. **Background checks are a status only**; the report never enters the system (FR-20).
8. **Volunteer hours are counted from check-in and assignments**, never typed afterwards, and a volunteer sees their own hours (FL-16, FL-17).
9. **A skill counts only when recorded**, never inferred from a role or a past assignment (PER-01, AC-05A).
10. **Badges never rely on the QR code alone**: name, agency, role and validity are printed and readable (AC-13C).
