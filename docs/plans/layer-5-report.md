## What did not pass, or is not yet proven

The first full stack run (G, H) then found a real defect: on an empty database, migration 0030 failed its dictionary check because 0041 had removed the entry for the column it later drops; local databases were already past 0030, and the drill only goes two steps back. Fixed in `12b1944` (entries for dropped columns load only while the column exists); verified by migrating a fresh database from empty, a 12-step drill over 0030–0041, and the whole stack started from empty volumes with the integration, database, enclave and browser suites.

## Readings taken in favour of safety and privacy (flagged for you)

1. The enclave serves its own screens on its own address and the console only links (ADR-0038), stricter than ADR-0021's first text.
2. A weapons hazard stays on the platform as an awareness flag without detail; weapon details live only in the enclave.
3. "Restricted: CJIS" is shown only in the console to people who can read the mission; phones, partners and the public see nothing.
4. The fingerprint-based check is required before access (not within 30 days); lapsed training denies access the day it lapses.
5. Screening records hold status and dates only.
6. No email or texts from the enclave; the one-time password is handed over in person.
7. CJIS records are kept 7 years after closure by default until the agency enters its state records schedule; legal holds override; the enclave audit is kept at least 7 years.
8. One agency owns each record; another agency sees nothing.
9. No break-glass in the enclave.
10. The first sign-in registers the passkey, but that session proved the password only, so the enclave refuses it and asks for one more sign-in with the passkey (fail closed).

## Decisions needed from you

1. Enclave screens on their own address instead of inside the console. Default: own address.
2. CJIS records retention. Default: 7 years after closure until the agency's state records schedule is entered.

4. Sharing a record between a sheriff's office and a city police department on one mission. Default: one owning agency, no sharing.
