# Layer 6 plan: FEMA package and official reports

Written 2026-10-14 after Adam merged layer 5 (Mennelabs/EOC-Application#8) and said "Start layer six" (`docs/decisions.md`). Build order SCL-14 step 6. Status record: [`layer-6-status.md`](layer-6-status.md). Everything from layers 1–5 keeps applying: RLS, audit of every write, every restricted read and every export, idempotency, versions, the error envelope, the data dictionary, retention categories, the confirmation dialog with a reason (RULE-14), synthetic data only (SCL-12).

Layer 6 turns what the platform already collects (verified resident reports, field assessments, needs, partner work, sites, debris tickets) plus public reference data into the outputs a county needs to ask for state and federal help and to brief its leaders: FEMA's own damage-assessment formats, the data parts of the governor's request (FEMA Form 010-0-13), situation reports and briefings that are reviewed, issued and frozen, shared metric definitions, and the equity dashboard. **The platform never submits anything to FEMA or the state**; it drafts, a named person reviews and issues, and the county hands the result over (DCL:DEC-14).

## 2. The counting rule every official number uses

One function in `packages/reporting` decides what counts, so the screen, the CSV, the PDF and the package cannot disagree (AC-14C, MET-06, GATE-07):

- **Counted toward degree-of-damage totals:** a report whose review state is `approved` **and** whose verification level is `desktop_verified` or `field_verified` (Section 29.2, DCL:DEC-03), read at its approved version (BRD:FR-12.05; a materially changed finding waits for re-review).
- **Shown separately, never added in:** self-reported counts; `cannot_verify` as the "inaccessible" total with its reason (DEC-17); witness observations (WIT-01; a helper's report about a specific home counts once approved and verified, because the home itself was checked: corrected 2026-10-14 during task C); pre-existing damage and non-primary residences, which stay in the record but leave the IA totals (DEC-04).
- **One home, one count:** reports linked as duplicates or superseded count once (BRD:FR-12.06); several reports for the same address key or parcel count once, the approved finding with the highest verification winning.
- **Multi-family counted per unit** (docs/05): a resident's report is one unit; a field assessment of an apartment building counts its recorded unit count.
- **Unknown is a category, never zero** (RULE-02): unknown insurance, unknown tenure and unknown home type are reported as such.
- **Low income is never per home** (NED-09, docs/05): the package reports the tract's ACS share and says so.

## 3. Reference data (ADR-0039, accepted 2026-10-14)

Free federal sources are the main feed; every dataset can also be imported from a file (CSV, GeoJSON, GeoPackage, zipped Shapefile) with a column mapping.

| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| Tract population profile      | Census Data API, ACS 5-year (free key, a secret)             | DEC-06 profile, low-income share, EQ-11 denominators, PA per-capita population |
| Structures                    | USACE National Structure Inventory API; FEMA USA Structures  | CON-07 coverage, homes by type inside the footprint, joint-assessment sampling |
| Flood zones                   | FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (ArcGIS REST)               | Narrative ("in a special flood hazard area"), map layer                        |
| Declarations, flood insurance | OpenFEMA                                                     | Prior declarations (narrative), NFIP coverage context                          |
| Risk and vulnerability        | FEMA National Risk Index, CDC/ATSDR SVI (import per release) | Equity dashboard context, narrative on special populations                     |
| Critical facilities           | CMS provider data; self-hosted OpenStreetMap                 | Outage log facility picker (DEC-07)                                            |
| Weather alerts                | National Weather Service API                                 | Event context, incident period suggestion                                      |
| Power outages                 | DOE/ORNL ODIN public feed (county level)                     | Outage log suggestions a person confirms (DEC-07)                              |

Rules: only geography leaves the system; each connector has an egress host allowlist; every pull or import is a versioned per-tenant snapshot with source, URL, vintage, retrieval time, licence and checksum; an issued report cites the snapshot ids it used; a failed or blocked source leaves the last good snapshot in place with its age shown (SCL-06). In development and CI the connectors replay **recorded responses in the real formats filled with synthetic WESTWIND values** (fictional FIPS `29999`, synthetic tracts over the five seed areas, structures derived from the seed parcels); a live smoke test against a real county is a manual command whose output is never committed (SCL-12). This build environment's network policy blocks the federal hosts, so live checks need those hosts allowed first.

## 4. Data model (migrations 0042 onward; every column in the dictionary, RLS on every tenant table)

- **Reference data:** `refdata_snapshot` (tenant, dataset, source, mode pull or import, scope, vintage, retrieved_at, url, licence, row count, checksum, status, error), `refdata_current` (the snapshot in use per dataset), and one table per dataset keyed by snapshot: `ref_tract` (GEOID, name, county FIPS, polygon), `ref_tract_profile` (population, households, renter households, median income, poverty, SNAP and SSI shares, age 65+, disability, limited-English households, households without a vehicle, language at home, margins of error), `ref_structure` (source id, point, occupancy, FEMA home type, units, stories, foundation, value, year built), `ref_flood_zone`, `ref_facility`, `ref_declaration`, `ref_nfip_summary`, `ref_risk`, `ref_alert`, `ref_power_outage`; `refdata_import` (uploaded file, mapping, validation result).
- **Geography:** `jurisdiction` (exists, unused) filled for county and city tenants; `location_geo` (location → tract GEOID and jurisdiction, with the tract snapshot used), maintained by the worker when a location gets coordinates or a new tract snapshot lands.
- **Declaration data:** `declaration` per event (incident type, incident period start and end as entered by staff, separate from the event's open and close; requested areas; state review label; drafts of Boxes 8 and 9 within 3,000 characters), `joint_pda` (scheduled dates, areas, teams, sample route), `outage` (facility or service type from the DEC-07 list, facility reference or description, area, start, restoration, source staff or ODIN suggestion, confirmed by), `casualty_count` (kind, count, official source, as-of, entered by; DEC-08), `pa_site` and its photos (applicant, location, category A–G, description, estimated cost with estimate-versus-verified flag, percent complete, insured yes/no/unknown, other federal agency yes/no/unknown; DEC-10, DEC-17). Per-capita indicators, equity and coverage thresholds and reporting periods are versioned `tenant_config` keys.
- **Official reports:** `official_report` (event, kind `sitrep | briefing | ida_package | pda_package | declaration_data | media_sheet`, scope, period from and to with time zone, cutoff, state `draft | reviewed | issued | superseded | withdrawn`, classification, author, reviewer, issuer, amends, form-mapping version, metric definition versions, version), `official_report_section` (key, kind `fact | judgment | narrative`, content, sources, edited by), `official_report_snapshot` (append-only, immutable: the issued content, the record versions and reference snapshots it used, a content hash, the object-store key of the locked copy), `official_report_export` (format, object key, who, when).
- **Form mappings:** `fema_form_mapping` (form, FEMA form version, mapping version, status draft/active/retired, fields mapped to a closed vocabulary of data elements, source document, approved by, effective from). Version 1 of each form is shipped from `docs/05`; later versions are edited in the console without a release (DEC-15).
- **Metrics:** metric definitions live in the existing `governed_definition` store (versioned, never edited in place). Proposed defaults (BRD:DEC-15): 12-hour operational periods starting 07:00 and 19:00 local; situation-report cutoff at the period's end; "closed mission" = state `closed` with a disposition (AC-14A); time to review = submission to first review decision; time to help = need created to `met`.
- **Equity:** `equity_alert` (event, tract or group, kind coverage / review gap / help gap, value, threshold, raised, acknowledged by, action taken).
- **Integrations (SCL-09):** `api_key` (name, prefix, hash, scopes, created by, approved by a second administrator, expiry, revoked, last used), `webhook` and `webhook_delivery` (signed deliveries of "report issued" and "package exported").
- **Field capture still missing (DEC-17):** field assessments gain flood depth at basement, first floor and crawl space in feet and inches, the can't-reach reason in sweep mode, units, insurance, and tribal ownership for tribal tenants.

## 6. Exports

- **PDF:** tagged, with a logical reading order, real headings and table headers, language set, and text for every image (NFR-12, ACC-49). The engine is chosen in ADR-0040 at the start of task F (candidates: PDFKit's tagged mode in the worker; headless Chromium printing semantic HTML; Typst's PDF/UA export). Every PDF is checked by veraPDF (PDF/UA-1) in CI, plus a manual reading-order check in `docs/a11y`.
- **CSV** opens in Excel (UTF-8 with BOM); this covers DEC-12's "Excel/CSV". Each file states its scope, period, cutoff, generation time, definitions and exclusions in a header block and a companion README (BRD:FR-14.05).
- **GeoJSON:** the PDA layer with FEMA street-sheet field names, documented so ArcGIS Field Maps and FEMA's validation tools can load it (DEC-12).
- Every export is audited with its scope and reason, carries the product name from configuration (ADM-03), never contains restricted fields or PII (DEC-13, SEC-10), and uses only EXIF-stripped, blurred photo derivatives that staff chose as representative (PHO-06/07/08).
- Issued snapshots and their exports are written to object storage with object lock (ADR-0010, not yet implemented; built here for snapshots and the audit anchors).

## 12. Readings taken in favour of privacy and accuracy (flagged for Adam)

1. **The counting rule in §2** (approved and verified only; exclusions shown, not dropped; per-unit counting; unknown never zero).
2. **No PII leaves in any package or export**, including to the state liaison and FEMA validators, unless a signed agreement names more (DEC-13); representative photos are blurred, EXIF-stripped derivatives a person chose.
3. **Low income stays a tract aggregate** from ACS; residents are never asked about income (NED-09).
4. **Casualties come only from staff entry with an official source**; residents' "anyone hurt or missing" answers are shown as unconfirmed and never added to official counts (DEC-08).
5. **Narratives are drafted from the data by template, labelled as drafted, and approved by a named person**; no AI in this layer (F15.02, CON-06 note).
6. **Reviewer and issuer are never the author** of an official report; issuing is its own permission (A7, Section 29.2 self-review).
7. **Issued snapshots are frozen twice:** append-only in the database and locked in object storage.
8. **Equity tables suppress any cell under 11** people or households (EQ-13); the tract profile is public Census data and is not suppressed.
9. **The incident period is entered by staff**, separate from when the event was opened in the platform; the 30-day clock starts from the incident end date a person enters (DEC-14, DEC-16).
10. **Reference data in the repository is synthetic**; only geography leaves the system when a real county pulls live data (ADR-0039).
11. **The outage and Census map layers move from layer 8 into this layer** because their data arrives here (CON-03).
12. **Helper reports count, witness observations do not** (task C): WIT-01 sets apart a third-party observation; a helper reports a specific home for its occupant, and once that home is approved and verified it is a damaged home like any other. Leaving helpers out would undercount the homes of people who needed help to report (older and disabled residents).
13. **Building-level findings absorb unit reports** (task C): a field assessment of a whole apartment building counts its recorded units, and residents' unit reports in that building are not added on top (one home, one count). An unknown unit count is counted as one unit and reported as unknown.
14. **Synthetic damage reports are opt-in in the stack** (`SEED_DAMAGE_REPORTS`, task C): the earlier layers' suites expect an empty review queue, so the seed files reviewed reports only when asked; the layer-6 tests file their own in their own exercise counties.

15. **Official reports (task E):** facts are never typed over (a wrong figure is corrected in its records, then the facts are refreshed); any change after review, including a refresh, sends the report back for review; a summary still as drafted from the data cannot be approved, so a person has read and saved every narrative (F15.02); the author can neither review nor issue, enforced by the authorization guardrail (G4) and again by the database; an amendment drafts its summary afresh from the corrected facts and carries the judgments over as written.
16. **Facts as of a cutoff** (task E): records made after the cutoff are left out, and those made before it are read as they stand when the facts are computed (the time is shown and frozen at issue with the record versions used); shelters keep no history, so their figure is "when the facts were computed".
17. **Locked copies are written after issue** (task E, ADR-0010): issuing never waits on object storage; the database snapshot is frozen at once and the worker writes the locked copy from the outbox, after checking the hash. Audit anchors move to the same locked bucket.
